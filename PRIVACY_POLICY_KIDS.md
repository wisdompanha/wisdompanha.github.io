# Privacy Policy for Khmer Alphabet (Kids Version)

**Last Updated: August 22, 2026**

Khmer Alphabet ("we," "us," or "our") is dedicated to providing a safe and educational environment for children. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the information we collect through our mobile application, Khmer Alphabet (the "App").

## 1. Our Commitment to Children's Privacy
Our App is designed specifically for children. We follow strict guidelines to ensure their safety and privacy, adhering to the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international standards for children's data protection.

## 2. Information Collection and Use
**We do not collect, store, or share any personal information from children.**

*   **No Registration:** The App does not require a user account or registration.
*   **No Personal Data:** We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, physical locations, or any other personally identifiable information.
*   **No Device Identifiers:** We do not collect persistent device identifiers (such as IMEI or Android ID) for tracking purposes.

## 3. Usage Data
We do not collect analytics or usage data. All progress made within the App (such as learned letters or quiz scores) is stored **locally on your device** and is not transmitted to our servers or any third parties.

## 4. Camera and Microphone Access
The App may request access to the device's microphone (for pronunciation practice) or camera (for specific educational activities).
*   **Microphone:** Audio recorded during practice is processed locally on the device and is **never recorded, stored, or shared.**
*   **Camera:** Any photos taken within the App are used only for in-app display and are **not saved or transmitted** outside of the device.

## 5. Third-Party Advertising
This app displays limited advertisements through Google AdMob. To ensure the safety of our young users, we have implemented the following:
*   **Child-Directed Treatment:** We have enabled 'Child-Directed Treatment' for all ad requests.
*   **G-Rating Restriction:** We restrict ad content to 'G' (General Audience) rating only.
*   **Privacy Protection:** These ads do not use interest-based tracking or behavioral modeling for children.

## 6. Third-Party Tracking
We do not use third-party analytics tools or social media plugins that track user behavior.

## 7. Links to Other Sites
The App does not contain links to external websites or social media platforms. Any parent-focused sections (like "Settings" or "Rate Us") are protected by a "Parental Gate" to prevent accidental access by children.

## 8. Data Security
Because we do not collect any personal information, there is no risk of your child's data being compromised on our servers.

## 9. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. We will notify users of any significant changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.
