# Privacy Policy for Mortgage Loan Calc

**Effective Date: August 6, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how "Mortgage Loan Calc" (the "Application") handles your information. By using the Application, you agree to the terms described in this policy.

## 1. Information Collection and Use
The Application is designed to be a private utility tool.

*   **Personal Information (Name and Phone Number):** The Application allows you to voluntarily enter a borrower's name and phone number. This information is used **exclusively on your device** to display on-screen and to include in the PDF reports you generate. We do not collect, store, or transmit this information to any external servers.
*   **Local Processing:** All calculations (loan amounts, interest rates, payment schedules) are performed locally on your device. None of this data is transmitted to our servers or any third-party servers.

## 2. Third-Party Services
We use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you.

### Advertisements (Google AdMob)
The Application uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may use device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID) to serve personalized advertisements based on your interests. You can learn more about how Google handles your data by visiting: [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## 3. PDF Export Feature
The Application allows you to export your loan repayment schedule as a PDF file.
*   This file is saved directly to your device's local storage in a location of your choice.
*   We do not have access to, nor do we store, any files generated by the Application.

## 4. Permissions
The Application may request the following permissions:
*   **Internet Access:** Required to load advertisements from Google AdMob and check ad status.
*   **Network State:** Required to check for an active internet connection for advertisements.

## 5. Security
We are committed to protecting your privacy. Since the Application does not transmit personal data externally, your information remains on your device under your control.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
