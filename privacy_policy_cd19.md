We collect several different types of information for various purposes to provide and improve our Service to you.

We may use and store information about your location if you give us permission to do so. We use this data to provide features of our Service, such as regional COVID-19 statistics and map visualizations. You can enable or disable location services when you use our Service at any time through your device settings.

We collect information about your mobile device, including the hardware model, operating system and version, and unique device identifiers to ensure the application's compatibility and performance.
